Open or Shut keeps one pretend door on the phone. Open it, shut it, lock it, or unlock it. A locked door stays locked until you unlock it. An open door must be shut before it locks.

The door stays on this phone. Start a new door when you want it shut again.

Open or Shut does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, a real lock, or a network connection. It is not a checklist for leaving home. The door in the app is original.
